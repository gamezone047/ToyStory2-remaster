Version: 2026.11.7.9

- New mechanism groups (ColliderSync.h) for levels 4, 5, 7, 8, 10 and 13. A group is a set of moving colliders plus the script values that drive them. The player standing on it owns it (the host on a tie); groups with a range also follow the nearest player, with hysteresis; otherwise the last owner keeps it. The owner shares collider position, rotation, pending movement/rotation, enable state and the group's values; the other PC writes them around its native level tick, and meshes, ropes and visuals follow natively. Taking over continues from the last shared state.
- When the other player presses, shoots or steps on something in a group they do not own, their game sends a one-time compare-and-swap request; the owner applies it only if its value has not moved on, so triggers from either PC work without rewinding progress.
